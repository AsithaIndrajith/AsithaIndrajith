# Bio

Asitha Kottahachchi is a Ph.D. student at [RMIT University](https://www.rmit.edu.au/) in Melbourne. He obtained his first degree, a Bachelor of Science (Hons) in Computer Science, from the [University of Colombo School of Computing](https://ucsc.cmb.ac.lk/) in Sri Lanka and then joined the IT industry.

After spending a few years in industry, he received a scholarship to undertake doctoral research at RMIT University. His research interests are in the domains of machine learning, quantum computing, and cybersecurity.

# Research Interests

- Machine Learning
- Quantum Computing
- Cybersecurity
